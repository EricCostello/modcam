ModCam

Your iPhone's sensor, before anyone has an opinion about it.

What it is

ModCam is a RAW camera for iPhone. It writes the sensor data your phone actually recorded to a DNG file and stops there — no smoothing, no sharpening, no scene detection, nothing added on the way out.

You get direct manual control of exposure, shutter, ISO, focus, and white balance, and a viewfinder built to tell you the truth about what the sensor is seeing rather than to flatter it. Captures go straight to your own photo library.

Requirements

iPhone 17 Pro / 17 Pro Max / 18 Pro / 18 Pro Max

ModCam needs the sensor and processing hardware on these models. It is an iPhone app and does not run on iPad.
